H12 model: values, sources and open questions

Key legend

Click a key to remap it, then press the new key. Esc cancels. Keys move a stick toward full deflection at the slew rate; the spring returns it. Hold Shift for half deflection. Keys drive the physical sticks, so the function follows the stick mode.

Gamepad (standard mapping)

Left and right sticks drive the RC sticks. LB / RB: switch E down / up. LT / RT: switch F down / up. Face buttons: A, B, C, D (bottom, right, left, top). D-pad left / right: wheel G. D-pad up / down: wheel H. Start held 4 s: power.
